1. **RSB Postdoctoral Fellow**, NTU, 2019

   Research Scholarship Block Postdoctoral Fellow (RSB-PDF) funds deserving post-graduate research fellows, providing awardees with the opportunity to advance their postdoctoral training under established faculty members and build their research career. I was among 25 recipients of the highly competitive RSB-Postdoctoral Fellowship, selected through a stringent process.
